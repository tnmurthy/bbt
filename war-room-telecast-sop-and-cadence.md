# Bigg Boss Telugu Season 10 — War Room Telecast SOP & Live Operations Cadence

**Document Classification:** Internal War Room Operations Manual  
**Operational Scope:** Daily 24x7 monitoring with high-alert Live Telecast Shifts (09:30 PM – 02:00 AM IST)  
**Applicable For:** Agency Core Team, Media Manager, Assigned Approver

## 1. War Room Seat Architecture & Shift Roster

During live telecast windows (Mon–Fri 10:00 PM – 11:30 PM | Sat–Sun 09:00 PM – 10:30 PM), the War Room shifts into **High-Velocity Mode**. Every seat has dedicated, non-overlapping responsibilities.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     WAR ROOM LIVE TELECAST SEATS                       │
├───────────────────┬───────────────────┬────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. LIVE LOGGER    │ 2. LEAD EDITOR    │ 3. COPY & MEME LEAD            │
│ Real-time log &   │ Rapid cut, hook,  │ Telugu punchlines, captions,   │
│ timestamping      │ 9:16 vertical crop│ thumbnails & audio trends      │
├───────────────────┼───────────────────┼────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. DISPATCH LEAD  │ 5. STRATEGIST     │ 6. MEDIA MANAGER (Client Side) │
│ WhatsApp Captains,│ Narrative filter, │ 8–15 min approval SLA & crisis │
│ Telegram & X posts│ triage & escalation│ escalation to contestant family│
└───────────────────┴───────────────────┴────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Shift Rosters & Hours
- **Day Shift (10:00 AM – 06:00 PM):** Sentiment sweeps, meme community coordination, unofficial poll tracking, daily scorecard logging.
- **Pre-Show Shift (06:00 PM – 09:30 PM):** Review Star Maa promo teasers, prep pre-episode excitement reels, verify WhatsApp broadcast queues.
- **War Room Live Telecast Shift (09:30 PM – 02:00 AM):** Live logging, rapid clipping (30–60 min SLA), immediate voting mobilization, overnight narrative seeding.

## 3. Weekend Telecast Run-Sheet (Sat–Sun Nagarjuna Episodes)

> **Note:** Weekend episodes air earlier (09:00 PM – 10:30 PM) and carry 3x emotional weight due to host reviews, task performance post-mortems, and elimination announcements.

- **08:30 PM:** Pre-show emergency briefing; all hands active.
- **09:00 PM – 10:30 PM:** Live logging focusing on host interactions:
  - *If contestant receives host praise:* Clip within 20 mins; frame as "Talent recognized despite house politics."
  - *If contestant receives host rebuke:* Trigger **Dignified Resilience Protocol**; clip the contestant listening respectfully without aggressive defiance; frame as "He will take the feedback and rise higher."
  - *If contestant is saved from elimination:* Trigger **Gratitude & Community Mobilization Blast** to all WhatsApp groups within 10 minutes.
- **10:30 PM – Midnight:** Weekend meme & editorial push capturing the emotional high point.

## 4. Rapid Approval SLA & Color-Coded Triage Tree

To achieve the **30–60 minute publishing SLA** without risking brand safety, content is triaged into three strict approval lanes:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        RAPID CONTENT TRIAGE TREE                       │
├────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ GREEN LANE (Fast)  │ Task wins, humor, wholesome clips, emotion.       │
│                    │ -> Pre-cleared, Auto-publish in <30 min SLA       │
├────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ AMBER LANE (Review)│ Heated banter, sarcasm, ambiguous reactions.       │
│                    │ -> Media Manager sign-off in 8 min SLA            │
├────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ RED LANE (Crisis)  │ Physical clashes, host conflict, sensitive drama. │
│                    │ -> Strategist + Legal + Family sign-off (15 min)  │
└────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Triage Lane | Asset Types | Required Approvers | SLA Target | Timeout Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Green Lane** (Fast Track) | Task wins, humorous moments, family love, emotional vulnerability, positive dialogue. | Lead Editor + Copy Lead | Instant / Pre-Cleared | Auto-publish. |
| **Amber Lane** (Standard Review) | Sarcastic comebacks, mild house arguments, contested task decisions. | Media Manager (Client) | **8 Minutes** | If no response in 8 mins, Strategist holds or uses softer edit. |
| **Red Lane** (Crisis Escalation) | Physical contact, abuse accusations, host confrontation, severe house clashes. | Media Manager + Family Spokesperson + Legal | **15 Minutes** | **Never auto-publish.** Default to silence until full-context review. |

## 5. Seat-Specific Operational Checklists

### Logger & Sentiment Tracker Checklist
- [ ] Record exact time-code and dialogue transcription of all contestant appearances.
- [ ] Tag moments with narrative category: `[UNDERDOG]`, `[TASK_BEAST]`, `[COMEDY]`, `[RESILIENCE]`, `[TARGETED]`.
- [ ] Monitor unofficial polling websites every 60 mins during voting windows.
- [ ] Check X hashtags (`#BiggBossTelugu10`, `#[ContestantName]`) for emerging smear keywords.

### Lead Video Editor Checklist
- [ ] 9:16 aspect ratio (1080x1920) formatted for Reels, Shorts, and Status.
- [ ] First 3 seconds MUST have a high-contrast visual hook or punchy Telugu text header.
- [ ] Accurate Telugu + English on-screen subtitles for every spoken word.
- [ ] Campaign watermark placed unobtrusively in top-right corner.
- [ ] Clean audio normalization with high-energy trending Telugu BGM overlay (low volume behind dialogue).

### Copy & Community Dispatcher Checklist
- [ ] Write 3 caption variations: (1) Emotional Telugu, (2) Short & Punchy for WhatsApp, (3) Hashtag-loaded for X.
- [ ] Ensure the dual CTA is intact: Hotstar step-by-step + Missed Call number.
- [ ] Dispatch to Captains Circle with a pinned broadcast note: *"Captains, forward to your district groups immediately with 'VOTED' challenge."*

## 6. Daily Emergency Escalation Tree

For urgent late-night incidents (between 11:30 PM and 08:00 AM):
1. **Level 1 (War Room Lead):** Evaluates severity within 5 minutes.
2. **Level 2 (Media Manager):** Reached via direct phone call (not text).
3. **Level 3 (Family Spokesperson / Legal):** Reached by Media Manager if legal/mainstream crisis holding statement is required.

*All night-shift personnel must have emergency phone numbers on priority bypass (Do Not Disturb override).*
