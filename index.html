Two ways this plan fails

Affection without organisation

Strong polls, warm comments, rising followers — and a flat VOTED check-in ratio. The most common way a liked contestant is evicted, and invisible unless someone watches the right number.

Detection: check-in ratio flat or falling two weeks running while sentiment rises. Response: stop making content, fix captains. The problem is never the content when this happens.

A pleasant season with no low point

Liked by everyone, in conflict with no one, finishes fifth. Nothing ever happened to him, so the audience never had to decide they were on his side.

Detection: by Week 8 never seriously nominated and no identified aggressor. Response: one real stand on a house issue — a genuine position, held publicly, that costs him something. Never manufactured conflict.

Ten rules for inside the house

Hand this to him before he walks in. After 6 September nobody can correct him for fifteen weeks.

01Take the task nobody wants, every week, and never mention that you took it.

03Say "I stopped getting work" out loud, once, early, without self-pity. Owning the decline disarms it permanently.
04Lose an argument gracefully in the first fortnight. Audiences distrust the housemate who never concedes.
