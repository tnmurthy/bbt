5 · Crisis Triage & Underdog Jiu-Jitsu Decision Engine

Automated triage framework for house controversies, negative smear campaigns, and viral troll spikes.

2-Hour Rapid Response SLA

Tier 1 · Troll Noise Low Threat

Isolated negative comments, low-follower Twitter accounts, routine opposition banter.

  • Action: Starve the fire — strict no-reply rule.
  • Block, report bots & document for internal audit.
  • Captains instructed not to engage or feed trolls.

Tier 2 · Trending Misinformation Moderate Threat

Out-of-context video cut trending on X/Instagram, rival fan club organized attack.

  • Action: Release uncut full-context clip within 2 hours.
  • Deploy Telugu quote card explaining contestant's true stance.
  • Underdog Jiu-Jitsu: Reframe attack as proof rivals fear our player!

Tier 3 · Mainstream Allegation Critical Threat

Host weekend reprimand, mainstream media news coverage, or serious personal smear.

  • Action: One official bilingual statement; all handles hold fire.
  • Family/Legal review before external dissemination.
  • Channel community outrage directly into verified Hotstar votes!
