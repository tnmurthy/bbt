Assumptions & Notes

Revenue logic

  • Pre-entry (2 weeks) billed as a fixed one-time fee
  • Retainer earned only for weeks the contestant is in the house
  • Survival bonus accrues from Week 4; finale bonus at W15; title bonus only if the win toggle is on
  • Paid media & influencer budgets are client money passed through at actuals — the agency books only the fee/markup as revenue

Cost logic

  • Costs run for pre-entry + in-house weeks + 2-week unpaid handover (per proposal terms)
  • Team cost = fully-loaded salaries (strategist, Telugu editor, designer, community manager, trend coordinator)
  • Default ₹1.8L/month ≈ 5-person Hyderabad war room; Lean preset ≈ 3 people
  • Overhead covers office, admin, internet, misc.

Honest caveats

  • All figures ex-GST; TDS/withholding not modeled
  • Season length and voting rules follow broadcaster announcements
  • Salaries are indicative Hyderabad market rates — replace with your actuals
  • Benchmarks used in pricing docs: Indian full-service SMM retainers ₹1–3L+/mo; BB Telugu S9 finalists (commoners) earned ₹15–20L; national post-BB endorsement benchmark ₹5–8L/post
