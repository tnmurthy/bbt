## 2. Creator Briefing SOP (Zero PR Footprint Protocol)

> **Golden Rule:** Audiences instantly reject paid PR that looks paid. All creator collaborations must look **organic, fan-discovered, and native to Telugu meme culture**.

### The "Organic Seed" Briefing Template:
1. **Raw Clip Asset Delivery:** Send high-resolution raw episode clips via cloud link with 3 distinct timestamp options.
2. **Angle Suggestions (Never Scripts):** Provide 2–3 creative perspectives:
   - *Angle 1 (The Comeback):* How the contestant stayed calm when interrupted.
   - *Angle 2 (Comedy / Relatability):* A funny one-liner or expression during kitchen duty.
   - *Angle 3 (Task Fire):* Physical endurance despite unfair odds.
3. **Caption Freedom:** Creators must write captions in their own signature slang (Telangana slang, Godavari slang, or Rayalaseema dialect).
4. **Mandatory Subtle Tag:** Always tag the official handle in comments or collaborator invite (`@[ContestantHandle]`).

## 3. Dark PR Detection & Influencer Verification Audit

Before disbursing payments or booking bulk creator slots:

- [ ] **Engagement Integrity Check:** Ensure creator has at least 4–8% engagement rate on video reels (avoid bot-boosted follower counts).
- [ ] **Audience Geography Filter:** Minimum 75% follower base located in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bangalore, or US Telugu NRI hubs.
- [ ] **Rival Exclusivity Clause:** Creators under active retainer for our campaign cannot publish paid attack videos against our contestant on behalf of rival agencies.
- [ ] **Strict Non-Disclosure:** Standard NDA requiring creator to never reveal agency sponsorship or campaign coordination.
