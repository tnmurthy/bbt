# Positioning Canvas Workshop — Week Zero
## Bigg Boss Telugu Season 10 · [CONTESTANT NAME]

**Purpose:** Lock the contestant's underdog positioning, catchphrase shortlist, and red lines before entry — because once they're in the house, this document is the only strategic input we can't change.
**Attendees:** Contestant (if pre-entry), 1–2 family members/management, campaign strategist, content lead, community head
**Duration:** 3 hours | **Output:** Filled positioning canvas + catchphrase shortlist + red-line register + audit task list

---

## The One Rule of the Session

The 24x7 live feed will show who this person actually is. We are not inventing a character — we are choosing which true parts of the person to amplify. Every answer must pass the test: *"Will unedited footage support this, or contradict it?"* If the family says "calm under pressure" but the contestant is hot-headed, we adjust the arc now, not in week 6.

Push past PR answers. When you get a polished answer, follow with: "What would their childhood friends say?" and "Tell me about a specific time."

## Part A — Story Mining (60 min)

Record with consent. These answers feed the asset bank, intro videos, and crisis file.

**Origin**
1. Where did you grow up, and what did a normal day look like at age 12?
2. What did your family sacrifice for you? Who sacrificed the most?
3. What were you known for in your school/street/village?

**The wound (relatability engine)**
4. What is the hardest period of your life so far? What did it cost you?
5. What have you been rejected from, failed at, or been told you couldn't do?
6. What do people get wrong about you when they first meet you?

**The spark (what the house will underestimate)**
7. Tell me about a time you were dismissed or doubted — and what happened next.
8. When did you stand up for something even though it cost you?
9. What do you do for people without being asked? Give a real example.

**Temperament check (honesty required)**
10. When you're provoked, what actually happens? Describe your last real argument.
11. What behavior in the house will genuinely test you — laziness, groupism, disrespect, mess?
12. Have you ever lost control in anger? What happened?
13. Can you stay silent when insulted? For how long?

**Motivation (authenticity check)**
14. Why Bigg Boss — the honest answer, not the interview answer?
15. What does winning change for your family, specifically?
16. What will you never do on that show, no matter the provocation?

**People and place (community fuel)**
17. Who are the five people who will cry if you win? What do they do for a living?
18. What's your hometown proud of? What local places, festivals, foods define it?

## Part B — Canvas Drafting (30 min)

Fill the canvas live on a whiteboard, then score.

| Element | Workshop question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| One-line story | "A fan describes them to a friend in one sentence — what is it?" | |
| Wound | Which struggle from Part A is most universal for a Telugu audience? | |
| Wound ≠ weakness | How do they carry it — silence, humor, work? (must match Q10–13) | |
| Spark | The single trait the house will underestimate | |
| Enemy (a force, never a person) | Politics / groupism / privilege / being ignored — pick one | |
| Proof moments | Which house situations will naturally produce evidence? | |
| Catchphrase | From Part C | |
| Primary hashtag | #[Name]Army or variant — check availability on all platforms today | |

**Scoring (1–5 each, as a group):** Is it true (footage will support it)? Is it defensible (survives rival PR)? Is it emotional (makes someone protect them)? Is it content-rich (produces clippable moments weekly)? Kill any positioning scoring under 16/20.

## Part C — Catchphrase Sprint (30 min)

Criteria: 3–6 words, Telugu-first, repeatable by fans, works as a subtitle, works in both anger and joy, no double meanings that rivals can mock.

1. Silent brainstorm — everyone writes 5 candidates (10 min)
2. Round-robin readout; group similar ones (5 min)
3. Shortlist 3 by vote; test each aloud in three tones: rallying cry, reply to insult, celebration (10 min)
4. Final 3 go to week-1 audience testing — poll the seeded WhatsApp groups after the premiere; the audience picks the winner by week 2 (5 min)

Also draft: the signature sign-off line for all community content, and 2–3 Telugu idioms/phrases the contestant genuinely uses in speech (mine these from Part A recording).

## Part D — Red Lines, Audit & Approvals (30 min)

**Red-line register (family answers in writing):**
- Topics never to be used in content (deaths, illnesses, relationships, finances, religion/caste sensitivities)
- People never to be named or shown (children, ex-partners, employers)
- Words/allegations that would trigger legal action if used by rivals (so we pre-prepare responses)

**Skeleton audit (confidential, strategist + contestant only):**
- Old social posts, deleted accounts, public spats, past relationships likely to surface, financial/legal disputes, controversial opinions voiced publicly
- For each item found: one-paragraph context note prepared in advance (feeds crisis protocol 4.1)

**Approvals matrix:**
- Designated approver name + backup, with phone numbers: ________
- Live-window SLA: 15 minutes; non-live: 2 hours
- Sole family spokesperson for media: ________ (all other family members refer calls to them — brief them this week)

## Facilitation Tips

- If the contestant is present, interview family separately for 15 minutes — gaps between the two versions are where the real story and the real risks live
- "I don't know" from family on Q10–13 is a red flag — get answers from friends before locking the canvas
- Don't let the room pick a positioning because it sounds noble; pick the one the live feed can't contradict
