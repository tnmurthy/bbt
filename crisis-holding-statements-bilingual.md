## 1. Operating Rules for Crisis Publishing

1. **Never React with Anger:** Statements must project unshakeable dignity, maturity, and fact-based clarity.
2. **Speed with Authority:** Pre-cleared T2 rebuttals go live within **2 hours** accompanied by full unedited clip context.
3. **No Rival Naming:** Attack the *narrative distortion*, never mention rival contestants by name.
4. **Underdog Jiu-Jitsu:** Reframe external attacks as proof that the contestant is genuinely strong and perceived as a threat.
