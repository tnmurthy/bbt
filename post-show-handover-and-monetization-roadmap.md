### Week +1: Gratitude & Homecoming Orchestration
- **Day 1–2 (Exit Broadcast & First Post):** High-production emotional gratitude Reel & Carousel (`"From Day 1 to the end, you were my family. Thank you Telugu audience ❤️"`).
- **Day 3–4 (Hometown Welcome Rally):** Coordinate with District Captains for a safe, enthusiastic airport/hometown welcome in their home district (Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Vizag, or Warangal) with live streaming.
- **Day 5–7 (Digital Asset Handover):** Transfer 2FA, Meta Business Manager ownership, YouTube channel credentials, and WhatsApp admin rights to the contestant's personal management team.

### Week +2: Shielded Media & Interview Tour
- **Media Shield Protocol:** Pre-screen all YouTube interview channels (SumanTV, iDream, GreatAndhra, Tupaki, etc.).
- **Interview Ground Rules:**
  - Zero mudslinging against fellow housemates.
  - Express respect for host Nagarjuna and broadcaster Star Maa.
  - Reiterate personal growth, humility, and gratitude for the underdog support.
