## 1. Background & Opportunity

Bigg Boss Telugu is one of the most-watched reality properties in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, airing on Star Maa with 24x7 and episode streaming on Jio Hotstar. Season 10 launches in September 2026, and contestant visibility during the first three weeks largely decides public perception for the rest of the season.

Once a contestant enters the house, they have no access to phones or the outside world. Every vote, trend, and fan community must be built and run from outside — by a coordinated professional team. This proposal outlines a full-season, outside-the-house campaign for [CONTESTANT NAME] designed to do two things:

1. **Survive every elimination** through a disciplined, high-volume public voting drive.
2. **Build a durable public brand** — follower base, narrative, and media presence — that holds commercial value after the show ends.

## 3. How the Show Shapes Our Calendar

The campaign runs on the show's fixed weekly rhythm:

- **Nomination day (weekly):** Voting window opens right after the nomination episode airs. This is our highest-intensity window each week.
- **Voting methods:** Jio Hotstar app vote + contestant-specific missed-call number announced on the show. Season 10 vote limits per method/day will be confirmed in Week 1 and reflected in all creatives.
- **Weekend episodes (host episodes):** Peak audience attention — used for trend campaigns and sentiment defense.
- **Finale voting:** Short, high-volume window requiring an all-hands push.

**Implication for the client:** our team works to the telecast clock, including late nights on nomination and weekend days. This is built into the plan and pricing.

## 4. Scope of Work

### Phase 1 — Pre-Entry (2 weeks, before/around premiere)

Once the contestant enters the house, episode footage is our only new raw material. Everything that can be prepared in advance, will be.

- Brand kit for the contestant: positioning line, catchphrase, bio, key story beats ("origin story" the audience can root for)
- Asset bank: intro video, photoshoot edits, 30+ ready-to-publish creatives, reel templates
- Channel setup/optimization: Instagram, YouTube, Facebook page, X handle — handle consistency, bios, highlights
- Voting explainer kit: step-by-step Hotstar voting visual + missed-call number template (number inserted once announced on show)
- War-room setup: content calendar, approval workflow with family/management, monitoring dashboards
- Fan-club seeding: founding members, group structure, volunteer captain guidelines

### Phase 2 — In-Season Weekly Operations (~15 weeks)

**Content engine (daily)**
- Live episode watching; highlight clips cut and published within 30–60 minutes of telecast
- 2–4 reels/shorts per day: hero moments, emotional beats, funny clips, catchphrase content
- Same-night response content for any negative narrative or controversy involving the contestant
- Weekend episode specials: recap edits, host-interaction moments

**Voting drive (every nomination week)**
- Voting CTA creatives deployed within 1 hour of nomination episode ending
- Missed-call number + Hotstar steps on every asset during the window
- Scheduled reminder blasts across all WhatsApp/Telegram groups through the window
- Influencer and fan-page amplification of the voting message

**Trending operations (X/Twitter)**
- Coordinated hashtag storms at fixed times (typically 6–8 PM) on nomination and weekend days
- Trend participation tracking and reporting

**Community management**
- District-wise WhatsApp/Telegram fan groups with volunteer captains
- Weekly fan engagement activities (polls, fan art features, watch-party prompts)
- Moderation guidelines to keep fan spaces positive and within platform rules

**Sentiment & narrative management**
- Daily monitoring of comments, fan portals, YouTube analysis channels, news coverage
- Counter-narrative briefs and response content within hours of negative spikes
- Weekly narrative review: what story is the audience seeing, and what do we reinforce next week

### Phase 3 — Finale Push (final 2 weeks)

- All-hands voting drive with escalated posting frequency and reminder cadence
- Regional pride / hometown-angle creatives and ground-level activation coordination (with family)
- Optional paid amplification surge (DOOH, hoardings in hometown, metro/transit media) — see paid media section
- Finale-week daily reporting instead of weekly

### Phase 4 — Post-Show Handover (2 weeks after exit)

- Exit interview / thank-you content series
- Handover of all channels, fan groups, asset library, and credential documentation
- Post-show brand roadmap: how the contestant converts the fan base into work (brand deals, appearances, casting)
- Final campaign report with full data

## 10. Terms & Compliance

1. **No artificial voting or fake engagement.** We do not purchase votes, run bot voting, or buy fake followers/engagement. This protects the contestant from disqualification risk and platform penalties. This is a firm policy, not a negotiable line item.
2. **Content rights.** Episode clips are used as short, transformative excerpts common to fan-media practice; accounts may occasionally face platform copyright actions, and we maintain backup distribution channels for continuity.
3. **Approvals & liability.** The client approves positioning and sensitive content. The client confirms they are authorized to act for the contestant and that the contestant's pre-entry commitments to the broadcaster permit outside campaigning (standard fan/family activity).
4. **Show dependency.** Schedule, voting rules, and season length are controlled by the broadcaster; plans adapt to official announcements, and material changes will be communicated within 24 hours.
5. **Confidentiality.** Campaign strategy, internal data, and group structures are confidential to both parties during and after the engagement.
6. **Exclusions.** On-ground events, travel, printing, and DOOH production are billed at actuals with prior approval.
